## 自主商務中的動態可驗證多代理人类忠誠迴圈模型與淨人類-代理分數

## 英文摘要
The rapid proliferation of Agentic AI fundamentally disrupts traditional customer loyalty paradigms. As AI evolves from passive recommendation algorithms to autonomous, goal-directed agents capable of executing purchasing decisions, the conventional understanding of consumer-brand relationships requires structural reevaluation. By synthesizing literature across human-machine teaming, consumer decision-making, and algorithmic trust dynamics, this paper demonstrates that traditional loyalty models fail to account for algorithmic bounded rationality and constructed autonomy. The authors introduce the Dynamic Verifiable Multi-Agent Human Agentic Loyalty Loop (DVM-HALL) model, formalizing brand choice via a softmax probability formulation where human emotional equity, agentic machine-experience utility, calibrated trust, delegated authority, and verifiable execution jointly determine selection. The model features recursive updating mechanisms to dynamically calibrate trust and delegation after each interaction. Crucially, the framework integrates a verifiable execution layer for DeFi and tokenized loyalty settings, incorporating execution risks—gas cost, slippage, MEV exposure, smart-contract vulnerabilities—as core predictors of agentic brand preference. Furthermore, the Net Human-Agent Score (NHAS) is introduced as an auditable, risk-weighted metric designed to measure human-agent alignment using human feedback, execution logs, benchmark comparisons, and verifiable receipts. A three-stage empirical validation plan spans controlled shopping experiments, multi-agent market simulations, and DeFi testbeds.

## 繁體中文摘要
Agentic AI 的快速普及從根本上顛覆了傳統的客戶忠誠度範式。隨著 AI 從被動推薦算法演進為能夠執行購買決策的自主、目標導向代理，傳統對消費者-品牌關係的理解需要結構性重新評估。本文綜合人機協作、消費者決策與算法信任動態的文獻，證明傳統忠誠度模型無法考量算法有限理性與建構自主性。作者提出了動態可驗證多代理人类忠誠迴圈（DVM-HALL）模型，通過 softmax 概率公式形式化品牌選擇，其中人類情感權益、代理機器體驗效用、校準信任、委託權限與可驗證執行共同決定選擇。該模型具備遞迴更新機制，在每次互動後動態校準信任與委託。關鍵的是，框架整合了 DeFi 與代幣化忠誠度設定的可驗證執行層，將 gas 成本、滑點、MEV 暴露、智能合約漏洞等執行風險納入為代理品牌偏好的核心預測因子。此外，引入了淨人類-代理分數（NHAS），一種可審計的風險加權指標，使用人類回饋、執行日誌、基準比較與可驗證收據來衡量人類-代理一致性。提出三階段實證驗證計畫，涵蓋對照購物實驗、多代理市場模擬與 DeFi 測試床。

## 原始摘要（English）
The rapid proliferation of Agentic Artificial Intelligence fundamentally disrupts traditional customer loyalty paradigms. As AI evolves from passive recommendation algorithms to autonomous, goal-directed agents capable of executing purchasing decisions, the conventional understanding of consumer-brand relationships requires a structural reevaluation. By synthesizing extant literature across human-machine teaming, consumer decision-making, and algorithmic trust dynamics, we demonstrate that traditional loyalty models fail to account for algorithmic bounded rationality and constructed autonomy. To address this, we introduce the Dynamic Verifiable Multi-Agent Human Agentic Loyalty Loop (DVM-HALL) model. We formalize brand choice via a softmax probability formulation where human emotional equity, agentic machine-experience utility, calibrated trust, delegated authority, and verifiable execution jointly determine selection. The model features recursive updating mechanisms to dynamically calibrate trust and delegation after each interaction. Crucially, the framework integrates a verifiable execution layer for Decentralized Finance (DeFi) and tokenized loyalty settings, incorporating execution risks -- such as gas cost, slippage, MEV exposure, and smart-contract vulnerabilities -- as core predictors of agentic brand preference. Furthermore, we introduce the Net Human-Agent Score (NHAS), an auditable, risk-weighted metric designed to measure human-agent alignment using human feedback, execution logs, benchmark comparisons, and verifiable receipts. Finally, we propose a comprehensive three-stage empirical validation plan spanning controlled shopping experiments, multi-agent market simulations, and DeFi testbeds. This framework provides the foundational theory required for brands to navigate the impending transition toward machine customers.
