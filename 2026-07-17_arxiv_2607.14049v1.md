## 深度互動：大型推理模型的高效人機互動方法
英文標題：Deep Interaction: An Efficient Human-AI Interaction Method for Large Reasoning Models

作者：Hefeng Zhou, Jinxuan Zhang, Jiong Lou, Yuxin Liu, Chaochao Lu, Jingjing Qu, Jie Li
發布日期：2026-07-15
arXiv ID：2607.14049v1
PDF：有

## 英文摘要
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning has significantly enhanced the ability of large language models (LLMs) to tackle complex, multi-step tasks. However, when errors occur in the reasoning process, current interaction approaches are limited: they either re-generate another response that may repeat the same mistakes, or users must laboriously flag the faulty step in follow-up turns, often resulting in the model acknowledging the error but repeating it again. This paper proposes Deep Interaction, an efficient human intervention mechanism for precisely correcting reasoning errors in LLMs. The approach enables direct editing of the original response, allowing erroneous parts to be corrected while preserving accurate reasoning steps. The edited CoT is then refined into a distilled prompt, which steers the LLM along the corrected reasoning path. Experimental results demonstrate that this method achieves over 25% improvement in correction success rate and reduces token usage by approximately 40% on STEM reasoning tasks compared to baseline approaches. The key innovation lies in preserving correct reasoning steps while surgically correcting only the flawed portions, rather than regenerating the entire response.

## 繁體中文摘要
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) 推理技術大幅提升了大型語言模型處理複雜多步驟任務的能力。然而，當推理過程中出現錯誤時，現有的互動方法存在明顯限制：要麼重新生成另一個可能再次犯同樣錯誤的回覆，要麼使用者需要費力地在後續對話中標記錯誤步驟，結果往往是模型承認錯誤卻又重複犯錯。本文提出了「深度互動」（Deep Interaction），一種高效的人工干預機制，用於精確修正 LLM 中的推理錯誤。該方法允許直接編輯原始回應，在保留正確推理步驟的同時修正錯誤部分。編輯後的 CoT 被提煉為一個蒸餾提示（distilled prompt），引導 LLM 沿著修正後的推理路徑繼續。實驗結果顯示，該方法在 STEM 推理任務上較基線方法提升了超過 25% 的修正成功率，並減少了約 40% 的 token 使用量。核心創新在於保留正確的推理步驟，僅對錯誤部分進行「手術式」修正，而非重新生成整個回應，這不僅提高了效率，也大幅降低了運算成本。

## 原始摘要（English）
The emergence of Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning has significantly enhanced the ability of large language models (LLMs) to tackle complex, multi-step tasks. However, when errors occur, current interaction approaches typically involve re-generating another response that may make mistakes again, or users laboriously flag the faulty step in follow-up turns that may get responses <You are right, I made a mistake here> followed by similar errors recurring. To address this issue, we propose an efficient human intervention mechanism for precisely correcting reasoning errors in LLMs, termed Deep Interaction. Our approach enables direct editing of the original response, allowing erroneous parts to be corrected while preserving accurate reasoning steps. We refine the edited CoT into a distilled prompt, which then steers the LLM along the corrected reasoning path. Experimental results show that our method achieves over a 25% improvement in correction success rate and reduces token usage by approximately 40% on STEM tasks reasoning compared to baseline approaches.
