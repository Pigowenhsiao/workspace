## 多領域低資源 OCR 的多專家路由：以滿文為例
英文標題：Multi-Expert Routing for Multi-Domain Low-Resource OCR: A Manchu Case Study

作者：Zhan Chen, Jiqiao Ma, Chih-wen Kuo
發布日期：2026-07-15
arXiv ID：2607.14041v1
PDF：有

## 英文摘要
Historical Manchu OCR must accommodate various visually distinct writing styles, including regular script, running script, and the semi-cursive chancery hand used in palace memorials, despite limited labeled data. This paper studies a multi-expert system that reuses checkpoints from an iterative fine-tuning process as domain specialists and uses a lightweight page-level image classifier to dispatch pages by visual style. When the checkpoint pool lacks a suitable specialist, an additional expert is trained for that domain. On three frozen test sets, the routed system matches the selected specialist for each style at two-decimal precision: 0.30% CER on regular script, 1.57% on memorials, and 4.83% on running script. The router achieves 99.3% page-level domain accuracy and matches the domain-label oracle at the same precision. Notably, two of the three selected specialists were not trained specifically for their final domain; only the running-script expert was trained with that domain as its target. The paper reports the evaluation protocol, router design, and per-page predictions to ensure reproducibility.

## 繁體中文摘要
歷史滿文 OCR 必須在各種視覺上截然不同的書寫風格之間進行適應，包括正楷、行書以及宮廷奏摺中使用的半草書體，儘管標記資料有限。本文研究了一個多專家系統，該系統重複使用迭代微調過程中的檢查點作為領域專家，並使用輕量級頁面級圖像分類器按視覺風格分配頁面。當檢查點池中缺乏合適的專家時，系統會為該領域訓練一個額外的專家。在三個凍結測試集上，路由系統在兩位小數精度上與所選專家匹配：正楷 CER 為 0.30%，奏摺為 1.57%，行書為 4.83%。路由器達到 99.3% 的頁面級領域準確率，並在相同精度上匹配領域標籤預言機。值得注意的是，三位所選專家中有兩位並非專門為其最終領域訓練；只有行書專家是以該領域為目標訓練的。本文報告了評估協議、路由器設計和每頁預測結果，以確保可重現性。
