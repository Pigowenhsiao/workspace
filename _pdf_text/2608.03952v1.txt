   To address this, we introduce TACT (Taxonomy-Aligned
Conversational Tutor), a framework for training and eval-
uating pedagogically adaptive conversational tutors using a
taxonomy derived from authentic ESL tutoring interactions.
Its student-move taxonomy describes the interactional func-
tion of a learner and whether an attempted answer requires
repair (Stolcke et al. 2000; Suresh et al. 2022; Lyster and
Ranta 1997). Its tutor-strategy taxonomy comprises 13 strate-
gies spanning interaction management, instructional support,
and language-focused feedback. It integrates general tutoring
functions, such as prompting, backchanneling, and affective
support, with instructional and language-focused moves, in-
cluding topic-contingent teaching, error flagging, hinting,
and direct correction (Shute 2008; Lyster and Ranta 1997).
